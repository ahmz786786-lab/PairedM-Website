# PairedM — Complete Overview

## What Is PairedM?

PairedM is the UK's first face-to-face Islamic matrimony service. It's a Nikah (marriage) platform that fundamentally rejects the swiping dating app model. The tagline is "Unite By Meeting."

The core principle: every single match must lead to a real, supervised face-to-face meeting — not endless chatting. It's positioned as the antidote to apps like Muzz and Muzmatch that encourage hours of swiping and messaging with no real outcomes.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Muslim dating apps have a fundamental incentive problem: they charge monthly subscriptions, which means they profit from keeping you single. The longer you swipe, the more they earn. The result is endless matching, chatting, ghosting, and time-wasting — with no structured path to marriage.

PairedM flips this model entirely. It charges per meeting, not per month. When you succeed, they succeed. There are no subscriptions, no swiping, and no messaging phase. Every interaction is a real, supervised meeting.

---

## How It Works: The 4-Level Meeting System

### Level 1: Supervised Video Call (£5 per person, FREE for sisters)
- First real interaction — not messaging
- A PairedM coordinator hosts the call
- Both parties give feedback after
- Quick, safe, low-commitment way to see if there's compatibility

### Level 2: Mosque Meeting (£10 per person, FREE for sisters)
- Face-to-face meeting at a local mosque
- Coordinator present throughout
- Local Imam available for Islamic guidance
- Structured, dignified environment

### Level 3: Family Meeting at Venue (venue costs vary)
- Both families meet at a chosen venue (restaurant, hotel, community centre)
- PairedM arranges all logistics — booking, catering, introductions
- Coordinator facilitates the meeting
- Identity verification happens at this stage (profiles are anonymous until Level 3)

### Level 4: Nikah Ceremony
- PairedM arranges the Imam, venue, and paperwork
- Coordinates Mahr (dowry) discussion
- Plans the Walima (wedding feast) celebration
- Full event coordination from start to finish

A coordinator is present in EVERY meeting — not optional. This ensures safety, respect, and accountability at every stage.

## Key Features

### Pay-As-You-Go Model
No monthly subscriptions. You only pay when you actually meet someone. This aligns PairedM's incentives with yours — they want you married, not endlessly browsing.

### Free for Sisters
The entire service is completely free for women. Zero registration fees, zero meeting fees. This removes all financial barriers and ensures strong female participation.

### Verified Profiles with Background Checks
Every profile goes through verification. No fake accounts, no catfishing. Identity is validated before Level 3 meetings, and profiles remain anonymous until then for privacy.

### Islamic Scholar Oversight
A board of 3 Islamic scholars oversees the service:
- **Mufti Yusuf Dhanka** — Croydon Mosque, South London
- **Moulana Sheikh Abdur Rahman Patel** — Imam across 40+ mosques in California
- **Shaykh Moulana Sulaiman Ghani** — Iqra TV presenter, Alkhaleel Institute

Each scholar has provided video endorsements. The entire process is Shariah-certified.

### Wali (Guardian) Involvement
For sisters, the service coordinates with their Wali (Islamic guardian) throughout the process. Family involvement is built into the system, not bolted on.

### UK Nationwide Coverage
PairedM operates across 50+ UK cities, matching Muslims regardless of location within the country.

### Coordinator Support
A dedicated coordinator supports each couple through the entire journey — from first video call to Nikah. They handle logistics, mediate discussions, and ensure both parties feel comfortable.
